### 1. Information Storage & Collection (Local Data)

**Cable & Charger Tester: Health is designed with a privacy-first approach.** All battery health estimates, charging speed logs, voltage, current measurements, temperature readings, and test results are stored **strictly on your local device** using internal Android app storage.

- **We do NOT collect, store, or transmit your battery logs, hardware metrics, or personal data to any external server or cloud service.**
- You can erase all stored battery statistics and health history at any time using the "Reset Health Data" option in the app settings.

### 3. Android Permissions Requested & Usage

To provide battery monitoring, charge alerts, and advertising, our application requests the following Android permissions:

- **`INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`**: Allows the app to initialize Google AdMob and process privacy consent forms.
- **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`**: Allows the app to notify you when your target battery level (e.g. 80%) or temperature limit is reached.
- **`ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK`**: Used optionally if you configure local Smart Plug charging limits over your local Wi-Fi network.
- **`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`**: Required on Android 13+ to allow AdMob to serve personalized or non-personalized ads according to your consent settings.
